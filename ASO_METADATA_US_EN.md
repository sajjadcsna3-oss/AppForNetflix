## Recommended positioning

This app is a movie discovery and streaming-availability guide for Mac. It does not play, host, download, or provide a subscription to movies. Its most defensible positioning is: **find a movie, see where it is available in the selected region, and open the provider to watch it**.

The working title “App for Netflix” should not be submitted unless the developer has written authorization to use the Netflix trademark. The recommended brand below is intentionally distinctive and avoids implying affiliation with any streaming service.

## Competitive and keyword rationale

The current US App Store leaders use these positions:

- JustWatch: “Movies & TV Shows” / “The Streaming Guide”
- Reelgood: “Streaming Guide” / “Watch Movies & TV Shows”
- Watchmode: “Find where to stream anything”
- Movie Tracker: “Watchlist” / “Films & TV Shows Diary”
- MyWatchlist: “Movies & TV Shows” / “Keep Track of What to Watch”

The recommended metadata competes on the same high-intent entities—movie, streaming guide, finder, watchlist, availability—while narrowing the promise to functionality actually present in this codebase. It deliberately does not claim TV-show tracking, recommendations, alerts, sync, free streaming, rental prices, social lists, or in-app playback.

No public source provides reliable live Apple Search Ads popularity scores or competitor keyword ranks. Those values require an authenticated Apple Search Ads account or a paid ASO platform export. The keyword set above is therefore based on current competitor language, Apple product pages, feature relevance, and semantic coverage—not invented search-volume numbers.

## Launch ASO plan

1. Ship this US English baseline and localize metadata only with native, market-specific phrasing.
2. In App Store Connect, monitor impressions, product-page views, conversion rate, downloads, retention, and proceeds by source and territory.
3. In Apple Search Ads, use exact-match discovery campaigns for `movie finder`, `streaming guide`, `where to watch movies`, `movie watchlist`, and `movie tracker` to obtain real popularity and conversion data.
4. After 3–4 weeks or sufficient traffic, replace low-impression terms one variable at a time. Keep the name stable while testing subtitle and keyword-field variants.
5. Run Product Page Optimization on screenshot order/copy. Test the “where to watch” benefit against the “discover movies on Mac” benefit.
